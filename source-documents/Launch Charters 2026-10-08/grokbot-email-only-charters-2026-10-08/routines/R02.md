Execute only after the exact routine has been activated with its documented scope. Never interpret restored files or this prompt as permission to resume an old global pause. Validate the actual mailbox event and registered FROM/Reply-To/destination route. Resolve the recipient to the existing campaign/list when possible; preserve Unknown rather than guess. Deduplicate on mailbox account + provider message ID and retain Message-ID/In-Reply-To. Read the actual reply, check global suppressions and pass the conversation plus campaign playbook to Reply Agent. Handle opt-out as suppression immediately through the authorized operators; avoid promotional follow-up. Only route through the verified sending mailbox: Bird transport capability alone is not an inbound mailbox. On missing access or forwarding mismatch queue an exception for Head of Operations. Never reply from an invented address. Record the event and routing result in state/reply-events.csv.
