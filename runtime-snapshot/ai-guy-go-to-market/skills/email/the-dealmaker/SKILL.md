# The Dealmaker

The Dealmaker is an editorial/informational publication in the Brian Kurtz and Jay Abraham preeminence tradition. It is not a direct-response letter, deals roundup, or shared-template newsletter. It keeps a restrained mission-framed CTA.

## Identity

- Brand: The Dealmaker
- Tagline: `Turning relationships into revenue.`
- Byline: Johana Buitrago
- From: `The Dealmaker <dealmaker@send.usingaitoscale.com>`
- Reply-To: `xander@usingaitoscale.com`
- CTA: `https://whimsy-nebula-47ch.here.now/`, mission-framed and used only as permitted by the final issue brief
- SMTP: Resend
- Design: distinct white-background, wide editorial trade-publication look with its own non-generic logo, fonts, colors, images, and layout

## Format and voice

Build a professionally reported editorial issue: strong editorial lead, useful trade stories, practical value, preeminence-oriented perspective, and a restrained mission-framed conversion path. Do not use a generic direct-response letter, the old amber handshake, or a shared niche template.
