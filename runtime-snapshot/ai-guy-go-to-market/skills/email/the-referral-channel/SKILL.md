# The Referral Channel

The Referral Channel is an editorial/independent-news publication: a point-of-view channel with a strong lead, dispatches, and newsroom energy inspired by Substack, Breaking Points, and Zeteo. It covers the referral economy and must look clearly different from The Dealmaker.

## Identity

- Brand: The Referral Channel
- Byline: Jay Bell
- From: `The Referral Channel <referral@send.usingaitoscale.com>`
- Reply-To: `xander@usingaitoscale.com`
- CTA: `https://whimsy-nebula-47ch.here.now/`
- SMTP: Bird
- Native article when a conversion path is authorized: `https://medium.com/p/f7d99f4f2f10`
- Design: distinct white-background newsroom/broadcast look with its own chyron-style wordmark, typography, palette, image direction, and spacious layout

## Format and voice

Use a strong lead story, opinionated framing, and shorter dispatches. It is not a dry six-card digest and must not borrow The Dealmaker's design. Keep commentary clearly distinguishable from reported facts. Preserve useful trade value and source-backed reporting.

## Conversion and compliance

The publication may retain a mission-framed CTA when authorized by the issue brief. Do not use `power partner`, `AI Integraterz`, or `Published by AI Integraterz`. Use the exact physical address, unsubscribe controls, ASCII-safe email HTML, and approved merge tags. Verify every source in a real browser. Draft only; Xander approves before any test or send.
