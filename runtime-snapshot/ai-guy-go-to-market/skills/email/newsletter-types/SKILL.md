# Newsletter Types

Newsletter type controls format, voice, design, and whether an offer appears.

## Types

1. **Editorial / informational**: a professional trade read with news and genuine value. The Dealmaker and niche/industry newsletters use this type.
2. **Editorial / independent news**: a point-of-view news channel with a strong lead and dispatches, inspired by Substack and independent video news. The Referral Channel uses this type.
3. **Storytelling**: one continuous narrative that sells through story. Main Street Affiliate uses this type; it also informs the story spine of Local Affiliate Pro. The Open Source is editorial/informational told through Donald Miller storytelling.
4. **Promotional**: openly promotional but story-led, inspired by Sendiva and StoryBrand. Local Affiliate Pro sits at the story/promotional boundary.

## Newsletter map

- The Dealmaker: editorial / informational; Brian Kurtz and Jay Abraham preeminence.
- The Referral Channel: editorial / independent news; Substack / Breaking Points newsroom energy.
- Local Affiliate Pro: story / promotional; StoryBrand and Main Street businesses taking back their power.
- The Open Source: editorial / informational; Donald Miller storytelling and an anti-big-AI superhero theme.
- Main Street Affiliate: storytelling; Story Sales Machine.
- Agency Insider, The Protocol, The Green Room, The People Person, The Daily Sun, and The Front Desk: editorial / informational; pure education.

## Offer boundary

Niche and industry newsletters are pure education. They do not contain a native affiliate card, bottom CTA, affiliate offer, or offer link. Their job is trust and sender statistics. Conversion happens elsewhere.

Flagships and Local Affiliate Pro may retain a mission-framed conversion path according to their own identity skills. Never force a shared template or offer structure across types.
