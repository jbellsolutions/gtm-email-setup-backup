# Local Affiliate Pro

Local Affiliate Pro is a single story-driven, mission-led promotional publication at the StoryBrand boundary. The local business reader is the hero. Its recurring spine is breaking free from the advertising treadmill and Big-Tech pay-to-play dependence through community, networking, and affiliate channels.

## Identity

- Brand: Local Affiliate Pro
- Tagline: `A practical shortlist of offers local businesses can put to work.`
- Audience: local businesses, agencies, and operators
- Byline: Jordan Reyes
- From: `Local Affiliate Pro <local-affiliate-pro@send.usingaitoscale.com>`
- Reply-To: `xander@usingaitoscale.com`
- Destination/platform: resolve before sending whether Beehiiv opt-in or outbound
- Design: distinct white-background, warm human storytelling layout with its own logo, typography, palette, imagery, and spacious structure

## Format and voice

Do not produce a numbered affiliate-offer roundup. Build a continuous, mission-driven story with a clear local-business protagonist, the problem created by dependence on Big-Tech ads, the shift from networking to an affiliate channel, practical movement, and a restrained next step. Use StoryBrand principles: the reader is the hero, the publication is the guide.

## Compliance and verification

Verify every factual claim and offer-related statement in a real browser. Do not fabricate programs, commissions, outcomes, or URLs. Use the exact physical address, unsubscribe controls, ASCII-safe HTML, approved merge tags, and no `AI Integraterz`, `Published by AI Integraterz`, or `power partner`. Images are prompts only; Xander generates them. Resolve destination and opt-in/cold-outbound framing before any send. Draft only; Xander approves.
