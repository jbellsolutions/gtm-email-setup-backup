# The Front Desk

The Front Desk is an editorial/informational gym and fitness trade publication. It is pure education and is not a universal template for other newsletters. Its job is trust and operating intelligence, not conversion.

## Identity

- Brand: The Front Desk
- Niche: gyms and fitness
- Byline: Mara Ellison
- From: `The Front Desk <frontdesk@send.usingaitoscale.com>`
- Reply-To: `xander@usingaitoscale.com`
- Design: its own distinct white-background publication look with its own logo, typography, palette, imagery, and spacious layout

## Content boundary

Use current, source-backed gym and fitness trade reporting plus practical education. Do not include a native affiliate card, bottom CTA, affiliate offer, affiliate link, or promotional conversion touch. The Front Desk exists to build trust and sender statistics.

## Compliance and build

Use email-safe HTML, ASCII-safe entities, approved merge tags, exact physical address `37460 Beacon Brick Road, Zephyrhills, Florida 33541`, and unsubscribe controls. No `AI Integraterz`, `Published by AI Integraterz`, `power partner`, false relationship claims, or banned wording. Xander generates images from prompts; the agent never generates images. Draft only; no send without approval.
