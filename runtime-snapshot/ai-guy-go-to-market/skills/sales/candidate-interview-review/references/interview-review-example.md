# Interview review reference: normalized comparison and evidence handling

## Recommended comparison columns

Candidate | Role / interview | Relevant background | Strengths | Weaknesses / development areas | GHL or role-critical tool experience | Other tools / CRMs | Customer or outreach experience | Organization / data handling | Communication / phone skills | Availability / commitment | Objective assessment | Recording caveat

Use candidates as rows and keep the column labels stable across interviews even when the interviewer asked slightly different questions.

## Evidence distinctions

- **No exposure:** candidate says they have not used the tool.
- **Awareness only:** candidate has heard of the tool but has not used it.
- **Exploratory exposure:** candidate tried a free tier or looked around the product.
- **Production experience:** candidate describes actual work performed in the tool, with concrete workflows.

Do not collapse these into a generic “CRM experience” field when the tool is role-critical.

## Client-facing aggregation

When the requester wants an objective overview and does not want candidate-specific observations in the email, say that the candidates were generally capable but lacked the required technical depth. Put detailed candidate evidence in the attached workbook. Mention poor audio as a limitation on evaluation, not as a candidate fault.

## Recruiting proposal pattern

Offer, rather than assume, additional sourcing: publish a job post, recruit from specified university groups or private communities, use job boards, and line up more interviews. Keep the proposal optional and concrete.

## Attachment and contact checklist

Before finalizing: verify To address, CC, sender identity, greeting, attachment filenames, requested Slack/channel follow-up, and whether the email tone is straightforward, businesslike, and mildly warm.
