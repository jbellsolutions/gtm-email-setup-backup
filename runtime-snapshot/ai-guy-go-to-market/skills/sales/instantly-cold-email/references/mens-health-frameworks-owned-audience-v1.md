# Cold Email Frameworks — Men's Health Providers (Owned Audience) v1

**Purpose:** 10 frameworks for per-lead personalization targeting men's health businesses: TRT/hormone clinics, peptide providers, supplement/vitamin brands, longevity docs.

**Campaign:** #3617053 "Coaching Owned Audiences" | **Added:** July 13, 2026 | **Leads:** 8,255 | **Senders:** Arianna, Anna, Grace, Penelope, Kaylee

## 10 Frameworks (email 1)

1. **"Who Already Has Your Patients"** — Gyms, BJJ academies, barbershops already full of their patients. Core adjacent-audience play.
2. **"The 100,000 Websites Problem"** — Differentiation. 100,000+ sites pushing same protocols. Winners are recommended, not louder.
3. **"I'm Your Patient"** — Founder story opener. Shoulder, knee, jiu-jitsu, two months. Pure credibility.
4. **"The Podcast Engine"** — Simple local show where doctor interviews gym owners. Every guest becomes referral partner.
5. **"The Competitor With a Podcast"** — Threat framing. Competitor building voice in their market.
6. **"Referral System vs. Referral Luck"** — Referrals happen by accident unless systematized.
7. **"The Dead List"** — Reactivation. Old consults, dormant patients, show/newsletter to re-warm.
8. **"The Ad Squeeze"** — Escape from ads. Men's health = restricted category. Build second channel.
9. **"The Authority Gap"** — Men follow people, not clinics. Put doctor out front.
10. **"One Clinic Per City"** — Exclusivity. Partner map works for one clinic per city.

## Follow-up Sequence (emails 2-4)

- **Email 2 (Day 3):** Different angle pivot. "Most clinics I talk to are one algorithm change away..."
- **Email 3 (Day 7):** Short check-in. "Did my last note land? Wondering if referral partnerships are on your radar..."
- **Email 4 (Day 14):** Breakup. "If getting the gyms, coaches, and barbershops to send you patients isn't a priority right now..."

## Rules

- 50-100 words. Aim 85-95. Plain text. Lowercase 1-4 word subjects.
- Never: price/cost, 10x/synergy, revolutionize/disrupt.
- End every email with a question.
- Never "somebody else" — always name the business type.
- Never use "Owned Audience Engine" as jargon. Describe mechanism plainly.
- For role accounts: `first_name = "Team"`. `company_name` from domain. `last_name` from company for API validation.
