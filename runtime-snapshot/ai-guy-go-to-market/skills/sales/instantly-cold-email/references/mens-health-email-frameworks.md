# Men's Health Email Frameworks — Owned Audience Campaigns

Captured: July 13, 2026 | Source: Justin's Men's Health Provider frameworks doc + session corrections

## Core Message (use this exact language as the engine)

> Most people don't find a clinic through Google. They find it because someone at their gym mentioned it, their coach told them to check it out, or their barber brought it up. Right now that happens by accident. We make it happen by design.
>
> We turn that from luck into a system. The gyms, BJJ academies, and barbershops already full of your patients, turned into active referral partners who send them your way on purpose.
>
> It's the oldest, most reliable, most scalable way to grow — as long as someone's actually curating those relationships.

## What NOT to do (learned the hard way, July 13)

- ❌ Never use "when a guy searches" or "Google search" language — it sounds like digital marketing, which is exactly what they're escaping
- ❌ Never open with "Hey {CompanyName} team" or "Hi FirstName" — rejected as templated
- ❌ Never use "another client" — always name the business type: "another men's clinic in a different city"
- ❌ Never use "Owned Audience Engine" or "Borrowed Trust Method" as jargon — describe the mechanism

## Correct opener patterns (randomized across leads)

No greeting. Drop straight into a natural 1-liner:

| # | Opener |
|---|---|
| 1 | After looking at what you guys do — solid work, clearly legit. |
| 2 | Did some digging — you guys are doing real work in this space. Respect. |
| 3 | Saw your work in the men's health space — you guys seem like the real deal. |
| 4 | After checking out a few clinics in the area, you stood out. |
| 5 | Your name keeps coming up — figured I'd reach out. |
| 6 | Been watching what you're building — impressive. |
| 7 | Looked into what you guys offer — this is the kind of clinic I'd send a friend to. |
| 8 | Noticed you've been at this a while — experience shows. |
| 9 | Took a look at what you're doing — respect the operation. |
| 10 | Heard good things about you guys — had to reach out. |

## Email body structure (after opener)

1. Core message paragraph (the mechanism — gym/coach/barber → system)
2. Angle paragraph (varies by framework — reactivation, differentiation, podcast, patient story)
3. One-line close: "It's the oldest, most reliable, most scalable way..."
4. Soft CTA: "Want me to send over a short version of what that looks like for {clinic}, plus a case study from another men's clinic in a different city?"

## Follow-up sequence (no greeting — same pattern)

**Email 2 (day 3):** "Sent a note earlier this week about the gyms and barbershops already sitting on your patients. Different angle — [pivot]. Curious if that's showing up on your end. No pressure."

**Email 3 (day 7):** "Did my last note land? Wondering if building a referral system is on your radar this year."

**Email 4 (day 14 — breakup):** "Last one from me. If turning the gyms, coaches, and barbershops into a referral channel isn't a priority right now, totally get it. If it ever is, I'll map it out at no charge — just to show the potential. Either way, good luck."

## Variable angle paragraphs (6 variants)

1. **Referral map:** "We build those referral relationships — the gyms, BJJ academies, and barbershops that already have your patients' trust, set up to send them your way."
2. **Differentiation:** "Most clinics just buy more ads and hope. The ones sleeping well built the second channel."
3. **Luck vs system:** "Here's the hard truth: those referrals are luck right now, not a system. A buddy mentions you at the gym, or he doesn't."
4. **Reactivation:** "Same thing applies to your old list. Consults that never booked. Guys who stopped coming."
5. **Founder patient story:** "Quick context: I'm in my 40s, and in about two months I got strength back in an arthritic shoulder..."
6. **Podcast engine:** "The fastest way to turn that into a system: a simple local show where the doctor interviews gym owners, BJJ coaches, and trainers."

## Subject lines (lowercase, 1-4 words)

Per angle, randomized: `your patients` | `partnerships` | `nearby` | `standing out` | `why you` | `referrals` | `a system` | `word of mouth` | `your old list` | `reactivation` | `from a patient` | `two months` | `podcast idea` | `an engine` | `a show`

## Hard rules

- Plain text only. No links, no bold, no HTML.
- 50-100 words per email body. Aim 85-95.
- One research detail per email max. Never two.
- End every email with a question.
- Never: price, cost, investment, unlock, 10x, synergy, leverage, innovative, cutting-edge, solution, game-changer, transform, revolutionize, scale your, disrupt, guaranteed.
- No "hope this finds you well" / "I wanted to reach out" / "I'd love to."
- No medical claims, no patient data references.
