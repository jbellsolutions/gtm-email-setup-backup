# Solar Power-Partner Workflow

Reusable session detail for Main Street Affiliates solar campaigns.

## One-page structure

1. Headline: build the network around the solar company.
2. Opportunity statement: trusted local businesses already speak with homeowners who may be a fit for solar.
3. Partner map with four tiers:
   - Tier 1: roofing, electrical, HVAC, remodeling, windows, insulation, batteries, generators, energy audits.
   - Tier 2: real estate, mortgage, home inspection, property management, builders, investors.
   - Tier 3: landscaping, tree service, pools, pest control, pressure washing, smart-home/security, insurance, restoration, tax/accounting.
   - Tier 4: homeowner associations, employers, credit unions, chambers, developers, local media, nonprofits.
4. Operating process: map → recruit → agree on referral path → schedule promotions → run email/text/newsletter/event campaigns → route leads → installer closes → pay commissions on closed sales.
5. Market snapshot table with metric, value, and source/method columns.
6. Projection block labeled illustrative, with assumptions shown separately from guarantees.
7. Offer block: five referral partners in 90 days and 5x return or no payment, only when explicitly authorized.
8. CTA and review note.

## Research gates

- Confirm recipient name, company, website, actual service area, and target geography from direct evidence.
- Search the service area before naming local businesses or inserting household/business/solar statistics.
- Register outside sources in the citation ledger when the document contains fetched facts.
- If the domain is unresolved or only an email address is known, do not infer city, market, company identity, or service area. Keep placeholders and state the gap.
- Distinguish a verified market statistic from an internal target-list count and from an illustrative projection.

## Reply-copy rules

Use a direct, confident positive-reply draft. “Thanks for the quick reply” is acceptable. Avoid “thank you for getting back to me,” apologies, sensitivity language, and Owned Audience wording when the actual offer is Main Street Affiliates solar partner acquisition. Explain the affiliate manager, partner recruitment, scheduled/orchestrated promotions, campaign execution, lead handoff, close, and commission model. Include the calendar link only in a draft unless sending is separately approved.

## Review and send gate

A cron classifier or positive label does not authorize sending. If the user asks to see the one-pager or draft first, save artifacts and state clearly that no reply was sent. Before finalizing a personalized document, replace every bracketed field or explicitly list the remaining unverified fields.
