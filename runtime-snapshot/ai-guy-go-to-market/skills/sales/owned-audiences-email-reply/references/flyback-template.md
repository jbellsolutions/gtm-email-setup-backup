# Flyback Template

Use when a lead who replied positively got a low-quality AI-generated custom plan and called it out in a second reply. They're not hostile — they were genuinely interested but the plan was disappointing. This is a recovery email, not a standard case study reply.

**Trigger:** Second reply contains "generic," "AI-generated," "template," "not relevant," or similar quality complaint about the plan. First reply was positive (wanted the plan).

**Goal:** Own the mistake, validate their frustration, offer a real call — not another automated asset.

## Template

```
Hey {Name},

You're right — that plan didn't do the work. I dropped the ball sending something that read like a template instead of actually looking at {Company}, and I appreciate you calling it out.

What I should have sent: a real breakdown of who in the {market} already has your {audience}'s trust, and how to get those rooms talking to each other. {1-2 specific examples of partner categories relevant to their business}. — the actual map, not a Mad Libs version.

If you're open to it, I'd rather just hop on a call and show you what the real version looks like. Fifteen minutes, no pitch — I'll walk through what a genuine partner map for {Company} would look like and you decide if it's worth more time. If not, I'll take you off everything immediately, no hard feelings.

Book a time here: {booking_link}

{SenderPersona}
```

## Key rules

- **Owning the mistake is mandatory.** Don't deflect or make excuses. Say "I dropped the ball."
- **Use specific details** about their business / market to show you actually know who they are now. Not generic.
- **Offer removal.** Explicitly say "I'll take you off everything immediately" — gives them control.
- **Sender persona** is the same persona who sent the original plan (extract from quoted reply).
- **No case study link** — they already got one. This is a relationship repair, not another asset dump.
