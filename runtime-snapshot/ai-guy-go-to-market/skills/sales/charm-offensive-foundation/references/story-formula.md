# The Story Formula, IMPACT, and the Six Persuasion Principles

Shared narrative + persuasion reference for podcast, PR, speaking, and proposal skills.

## The story arc: Identity → Struggle → Discovery → Surprise

When you introduce yourself in a pitch (podcasts, PR, speaking) or open a compelling narrative, run
the reader through four beats. This is the engine behind Jon Buchan's famous "drunk cold email" story.

1. **Identity** — who you were / what you did. ("I used to run a digital marketing agency.")
2. **Struggle** — the honest problem. ("One year in, my word-of-mouth leads dried up. Desperate and
   drunk, I wrote an absurd email.")
3. **Discovery** — what you found / what happened. ("I sent 6 manually. Within a day, replies. Senior
   people at global brands wanted to meet. The email should in no way have worked. Yet it did.")
4. **Surprise** — the twist that makes it memorable and creates curiosity. (The ferret in bunny ears.
   "I don't even personalise beyond the first name…")

Keep curiosity alive — make them scroll, tease the mechanism, then reveal. End with the pattern
interrupt (the absurd image / prop).

## The "So why the hell am I writing to you?" list

After the story, pivot to a numbered list of why you're reaching out. Always land on **five** bullets
(the 5-bullet rule). Structure:

1. Genuine compliment / reason you chose them ("I really like your podcast / event / publication").
2. Why you'd be a good fit (your reasoning — proof you can deliver).
3. What you can offer *their* audience (useful + entertaining, and ideally unique).
4. Your mission (the bigger why — not about money).
5. A self-aware joke about needing a fifth bullet. ("I can't end this list on number 4. Five bullets
   just looks and feels better.")

## IMPACT → Communicate → Persuade

A core advertising lesson for any proposal or pitch. Most people start by *communicating* their offer
or trying to *persuade*. But without **IMPACT**, nobody is listening. Order of operations:

1. **Impact** — win attention first (charm, story, a striking idea).
2. **Communicate** — now you have the chance to explain the offer clearly.
3. **Persuade** — only then does persuasion land. → Success.

## The six persuasion principles (Cialdini)

Weave these into proposals, pitches, and follow-ups:

- **Reciprocity** — give first (free ideas, a mini audit, an interesting deliverable). Creates the
  obligation to give back.
- **Consistency / Commitment** — ask for a small initial yes that can be extended (trial, mini
  project, one call).
- **Scarcity** — a time-limited window or discount for working together.
- **Liking** — show you (and your team) are fun to work with; surface shared values or interests.
- **Authority** — case studies, testimonials, publications you've been featured in, awards.
- **Consensus / Social proof** — other clients (especially recognisable ones), client logos, numbers.

## Applying it

- Podcast/PR/speaking pitch = Story arc (Identity→Struggle→Discovery→Surprise) + "So why the hell…"
  5-bullet list + warm sign-off.
- Proposal = IMPACT first, then Communicate the plan, then Persuade with the six principles + tiered
  pricing (anchoring) + testimonials.
