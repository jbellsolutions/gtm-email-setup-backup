# Cold Email Frameworks — Men's Health & Hormone Clinics (v2)

**Purpose:** frameworks for an AI agent to personalize per lead after research. Cold outbound to men's health / hormone / TRT clinic owners and medical directors. The email is about one thing: **there are businesses in your city already serving the exact men you want — let me get them to send you their customers. Jay Abraham style.** The podcast and personal brand are *how* the partnerships get built.

**Niche:** Men's Health & Hormone / TRT Clinics
**Offer:** I set up joint-venture partnerships — the local businesses already sitting on your ideal patients, arranged to refer to you — using the Jay Abraham strategy I trained under him on, brought to local clinics. A simple podcast / adjacent brand is the engine that makes partners want to promote you. **I'll build the plan out for you at no charge, just to show you the potential.**
**Positioning:** The partnership guy. Not another ad vendor. Someone who's actually a patient too, trained under Jay Abraham, bringing big-company strategy to local clinics.
**Word count:** 50–100 words. Aim 85–95.
**Tone:** Plain text. Lowercase subjects. Sounds like a real person who did their homework.

## BEST PRACTICES — RULES FOR EVERY FRAMEWORK

**Subject lines:** 1–4 words · lowercase · looks like a peer/partner sent it · vague · never sell in it.

**Body structure (4 variables):** 1. Relevance — one detail from research (their city, service, ad). 2. Pain — you're competing on ads against every other clinic; nobody's feeding you patients. 3. Value prop — JV partnerships (Jay Abraham), with the podcast/adjacent brand as the engine. Optionally the founder's own patient story. 4. Soft CTA — a question. Never ask for a call on first touch.

**Hard rules:** 50–100 words. Aim 85–95. Never use: price, cost, investment, spend, budget, unlock, 10x, synergy, leverage, innovative, cutting-edge, solution, game-changer, transform, scale your, disrupt. The only "money" language allowed is the free-build offer ("at no charge, just to show you the potential"). Never mention performance pay or "no money until it works." Plain text. No links, bold, images. No "hope this finds you well." 8th-grade level. End with a question. One research detail per email. Never give medical advice or reference patient data. Do not pitch "category of one," "extract your genius," or personal-brand-guru language. Keep it about partnerships and patients.

## NICHE LANGUAGE GUIDE

**Use:** TRT, testosterone optimization, HRT, hormone panel, peptides, men's health, low-T, energy/performance/recovery, membership/cash-pay, concierge, referral partners, host-beneficiary.

## THE PARTNER-INVITE MECHANIC

The podcast isn't content — it's the JV engine. You don't pitch a partner; you invite them onto a stage. "Can I feature you on the show?" gets a far higher yes than "want to swap referrals?" — in our Power Partner Engine, that reframe moves replies from ~1% to ~22%. Every guest is a gym owner, coach, or barber already sitting on your patients, and once they're on your show the referral relationship builds itself.

## SELECTION LOGIC

| Signal | Framework |
|---|---|
| Clear local competitors / ad-heavy | #1 Who Already Has Your Patients |
| Owner values strategy / business-minded | #2 The Jay Abraham Play |
| No podcast/social presence | #3 The Podcast That Makes Partners Come to You |
| Owner is the practicing doctor / relatable | #4 I'm Your Patient |
| Cold, skeptical, wants proof not pitch | #5 I'll Build the Plan Free |
| Growth-focused, riding the TRT wave | #6 Demand's There, Distribution Isn't |
| No signal | Default #1 or #4 |
