# Cold Email Frameworks — RevOps for PR Agencies (v3)

**Purpose:** 10 frameworks built for an AI agent to personalize per lead after deep research. Cold outbound to PR agencies (traditional PR, media relations, executive thought leadership shops).

**Niche:** PR Agencies / Media Relations / Executive Thought Leadership Shops
**Offer:** RevOps for PR agencies (white-label backend). $15/hr, part-time (20 hrs/wk) or full-time (40 hrs/wk). Growth operator plugged into client work as hidden person under their brand.
**Positioning:** Backend partner. Their team's hidden person. Never a competitor.
**Word count:** 50–100 words per email.
**Tone:** Plain text. Lowercase subject lines. Sounds like a person, not a marketer.

## Framework Selection Logic

| Signal Found in Research | Use Framework |
|---|---|
| Client roster heavy on founders / execs / B2B SaaS, no podcast service on nav | #1 Podcast Service They Keep Asking For |
| "Thought leadership" or "executive visibility" on services page | #2 LinkedIn Layer You Don't Run |
| Newsletter mentioned as a deliverable but no proof they ship one weekly | #3 Newsletter Clients Want, You Can't Build |
| Leading with media relations, recent blog about HARO/Featured/reply rates | #4 Journalist Reply Rates Are Done |
| Reporting deck still shows AVE/EMV/SOV; no AI citation metrics | #5 AVE Is Dead, New Dashboard |
| Clients posted about hiring fractional comms / Head of Comms | #6 Fractional Comms Threat |
| Already pitching podcasts but visible volume is small | #7 White-Label SpeakerAgent |
| Growing roster, traditional retainer model, no recurring execution layer | #8 Recurring Revenue Line |
| Heavy exec roster, technical founders, traditional press tour pitching | #9 Exec Who Hates Media Training |
| Hiring "LinkedIn Manager" / "Podcast Booker" / "GEO Lead" | #10 Build It Once |
| No clear signal | Default to #7 (SpeakerAgent) |

## ⭐ Framework #7: White-Label SpeakerAgent — No Cap Pitching

**Angle:** In-house podcast booking caps at 10–20 pitches per client because it's hand-built. I plug a growth operator in from my team running SpeakerAgent.ai under the agency's brand. No cap.

**Subject line options:** `no cap` | `podcast engine` | `your bookings`

**Template:**
> {greeting} {first_name},
>
> Saw {signal_detail}.
>
> Honest ceiling on most agency podcast booking is 10–20 pitches per client per month because every pitch is hand-built. Client maxes out, then you're back to the spreadsheet.
>
> Let me take it off your plate. I plug a growth operator in from my team running SpeakerAgent.ai as your hidden person under your brand — part-time or full-time. We architect and manage. No cap on shows pitched.
>
> {cta}

## Hard rules (all frameworks):
- 50–100 words. Aim 85–95.
- Never use: pay, price, cost, investment, spend, budget, unlock, 10x, synergy, leverage, innovative, cutting-edge, solution, game-changer, transform, revolutionize, scale your, disrupt
- ("fee" is OK when describing PR retainer fees the agency charges)
- No HTML formatting. Plain text only.
- Lowercase subject lines, 1-4 words
- Every email contains at least one specific detail from research
- Position as backend partner — hidden person under their brand
- "I" first. "My team" when describing how the work gets done.
- Soft CTA — ask a question. Never ask for a call on first touch.

## PR Agencies-Specific Language

Use: earned media, hits, placements, byline, op-ed, beat reporter, pitch angle, tier 1/2/3, embargo, exclusive, HARO, Qwoted, Featured, SourceBottle, #JournoRequest, BuzzStream, Muck Rack, Cision, Prowly, CoverageBook, AVE, EMV, MIV, SOV, ghostwriting, thought leadership, exec visibility, executive comms, narrative, reputation, Forbes Councils, podcast guesting, dark social, GEO, AI Overviews, AI citations, LLM brand mentions, fractional comms, EVP, retainer, white-label, co-branded, backend, hidden person, sub-contract

Never use: unlock, 10x, revolutionary, synergy, leverage, innovative, cutting-edge, solution, transform, game-changer, scale your, disrupt

## Follow-Up Sequence

**Email 2 (Day 3):** Different angle. Not chasing — just curious if a second pain point is showing up.
**Email 3 (Day 7):** Short bump. "did the note from last week land?"
**Email 4 (Day 14):** Breakup. "I'll leave you alone after this"
