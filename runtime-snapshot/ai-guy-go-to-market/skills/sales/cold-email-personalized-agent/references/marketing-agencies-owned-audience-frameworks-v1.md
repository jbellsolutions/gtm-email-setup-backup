# Owned Audience Frameworks — Marketing Agencies v1

**Purpose:** 10 frameworks built for an AI agent to personalize per lead after deep research. Straight cold outbound to founders and owners of marketing, paid media, SEO, content, and growth agencies. The email is about one thing: **there are businesses, podcasts, newsletters, and communities already sitting on the exact clients this agency wants — I get them to send the agency their people.**

**Offer being sold:** I set up partnerships with the people who already have their ideal clients' trust — podcast hosts, newsletter operators, consultants, complementary vendors, communities — arranged to send the agency clients. A simple show or newsletter is the engine that makes partners want to promote them.

**Positioning:** The partnership guy. A peer — ran his own agency for 15 years — not another lead-gen vendor.

**Key constraints:**
- Word count: 50–100 words (aim 85–95)
- Plain text, lowercase subject lines (1–4 words)
- First person "I"
- 8th grade reading level
- Never ask for a call on first touch
- End with a question, always
- Exactly ONE research detail per email

**Banned words:** price, cost, investment, spend, budget, unlock, 10x, synergy, leverage, innovative, cutting-edge, solution, game-changer, transform, revolutionize, scale your, disrupt, guaranteed, "hope this finds you well", "I wanted to reach out"

**Framework selection logic:**

| Research Finding | Best Framework |
|---|---|
| No visible channel besides founder outbound and word of mouth | #1: Who Sends You Clients Now |
| Grew on referrals; case studies say "came to us through a referral" | #2: Referral Luck vs Referral System |
| Tightly niched agency, no podcast or show of their own | #3: The Show That Fills the Pipeline |
| Competitive niche; competitor has a show/newsletter; lost-pitch signal | #4: The Agency the Client Already Knew |
| Founder personally running sales, posting about being stretched | #5: The Founder Is the Funnel |
| Services page identical to rivals; generalist positioning | #6: Sea of Sameness |
| Agency 8+ years old — big CRM of past clients and dead proposals | #7: The List You Already Own |
| Clear ICP with obvious adjacent vendors and communities | #8: The Adjacent Audience Map |
| Serves referral-dependent clients (local, home services, health) | #9: Sell It to Your Clients |
| Strong client work, zero owned audience of their own | #10: Cobbler's Kids |
| No clear signal | Default to #1 or #5 |

**Email structure (4 variables, in order):**
1. Relevance — one sentence proving this email was meant for them specifically (from research)
2. Pain — call out their exact situation in their words
3. Value prop — describe the mechanism plainly: partnerships with the people who already have their ideal clients' trust
4. Soft CTA — a question. Never ask for a call on first touch. Never end on a pitch.

**CTA rules:** At least 6 of 10 examples should offer to send a short version of the plan plus a case study. NEVER say "another client" — always name the type: "another agency owner," "another SEO shop," "another niched agency."

**Follow-up sequence:**
- Email 2 (Day 3): Different angle
- Email 3 (Day 7): Short bump (did that last note land?)
- Email 4 (Day 14): Breakup (polite, final)

**Research checklist before writing:**
1. Agency niche and ICP
2. Founder LinkedIn — posting/selling? recent posts about churn, pipeline, referrals?
3. Case studies — how do clients arrive?
4. Agency age
5. Team size — who does sales besides the founder?
6. Do they have a podcast, newsletter, or YouTube show? (usually no = the opening)
7. Do competitors in their niche have a show or newsletter?
8. Services page — same offers as every rival?
9. Client type's adjacent vendors
10. Any public signal of a lost pitch, churn wave, layoffs, or pivot

**Niche language:** retainer, churn, MRR, pipeline, niche down, ICP, book of clients, RFP, discovery call, closed-lost, referral partner, founder-led sales, sea of sameness

**Partner types to name in emails:** podcast hosts, newsletter operators, complementary agencies/dev shops, bookkeepers/CFOs, business brokers, SaaS platforms, industry associations, masterminds, Slack/Skool communities, consultants one lane over
