# Cold Email Frameworks — The In-House AI Guy for Coaches (v1)

**Horizontal:** Coaching businesses — business, health, relationship, executive, life coaches.
**Offer:** Place one trained AI operator in-house to run automation AND go-to-market.
**Positioning:** Not an agency, not a VA, not a course. One in-house person who owns the outcome. Trained for coaches.
**Proof:** Josh at School for Men — ~2 years running, still no churn.

## Offer Summary

We place a trained AI operator inside your coaching business — in-house, working only for you. They connect all your tools, automate the busywork off your plate, and run your go-to-market: content, podcasts, joint ventures, power-partner referrals, and new channels. One person, using AI — instead of stacking an agency, a media buyer, and a consultant. Trained specifically for coaching businesses by someone who's been in the coaching world for years.

## Rules (applied to every framework)

### Subject Lines
- 1–4 words · lowercase · looks like a peer sent it · vague · never sell in it.

### Body Structure (4 variables)
1. **Relevance** — one detail from research (their niche, channel, podcast, offer).
2. **Pain** — the growth/GTM gap: great coach, but the marketing side eats the week or doesn't happen.
3. **Value prop** — one in-house AI person who runs [that specific GTM lever] + your automations. Trained for coaches.
4. **Soft CTA** — a question that invites a reply, usually offering to send the Josh/others-like-you case study.

### Hard Rules
- 50–100 words. Aim 85–95.
- **Never use:** price, cost, pay, spend, budget, investment, unlock, 10x, synergy, leverage, innovative, cutting-edge, solution, game-changer, transform, scale your, disrupt, ROI.
- Reference the agency stack without money words: "instead of juggling an agency, a media buyer, and a consultant."
- Plain text. No links, bold, images. No "hope this finds you well." 8th-grade level. End with a question.
- One research detail per email. "I" first; "my team" for how the work gets done.
- Every email carries the AI-guy-in-house idea and offers to send the case study on reply.

### Coaching Niche Language
**Use:** clients, calls, offers, program, mastermind, cohort, launch, funnel, follow-up, content, podcast guesting, joint venture, power partner, referral, in-house, your tools, off your plate, go-to-market, get and keep clients.
**Never:** banned list; generic marketing agency terms ("omnichannel," "growth hacking," "performance marketing").

## Framework Selection Logic

| Signal in Research | Framework |
|---|---|
| Would shine on podcasts, no guest spots visible | #1 Get You On Podcasts |
| No partnerships / isolated | #2 Joint Ventures |
| Ad-reliant, wants referrals | #3 Power Partners |
| Clearly one dominant channel | #4 Open Another Channel |
| Visibly using agencies / multiple vendors | #5 One Person Instead of the Stack |
| Messy tool stack / solo overwhelmed | #6 Connect Your Tools |
| Skeptical of generic AI/VAs | #7 Trained for Coaches |
| Strong coach, weak marketing presence | #8 You Coach, We Run Growth |
| Very lean / solo operator | #9 Three-Person Dream Team |
| Cold, needs proof first | #10 The Josh Story |
| No clear signal | Default #8 or #10 |
